# Portfolio GRC Analyst

This repository contains practical Governance, Risk and Compliance projects focused on cybersecurity risk management, IT governance, audit readiness and security documentation.

The objective of this portfolio is to demonstrate practical skills in identifying risks, assessing controls, documenting findings and communicating cybersecurity risk to management.

## Projects

### Project 01 — Cyber Risk Register

A cybersecurity risk register for a fictional European telehealth company, MediConnect Health S.r.l.

The project includes:

- Company profile
- Risk methodology
- Critical assets and business processes
- Cyber risk register
- Inherent and residual risk scoring
- Current controls
- Recommended treatment plans
- Executive summary

[Open Project 01](./Project-01-Cyber-Risk-Register)
